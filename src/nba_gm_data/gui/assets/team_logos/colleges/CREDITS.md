# College Logo Credits

The school marks in this folder are sourced from ESPN's college basketball team-logo CDN (`https://a.espncdn.com/i/teamlogos/ncaa/500/{team-id}.png`) and reduced locally to 96px images for the prospect list. These marks belong to their respective schools; this project is not affiliated with ESPN or the schools.
