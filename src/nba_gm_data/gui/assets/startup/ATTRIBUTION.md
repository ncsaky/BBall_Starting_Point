# Save-screen basketball photography

The following Wikimedia Commons photographs are used as the source material for the transparent, dot-pressed artwork in `press/`. The visible players and balls are retained as photographed; arena and crowd backgrounds are removed, and the complete foreground action group is scaled to a consistent display height without cropping.

## User-supplied photographs

The following photographs were provided directly by the user for local visual prototyping. The project has not verified their original publishers or reuse rights; the Getty-labeled image in particular must not be redistributed without appropriate clearance. All were background-removed, normalized to a consistent player height, and converted to monochrome halftone artwork. Visible basketballs and selected hoop/net details are preserved where they appear in the source.
