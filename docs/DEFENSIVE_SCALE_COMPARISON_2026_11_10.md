## Scale and consumer changes

Previously DEF displayed its direct skill grade while OFF, OVR and other tools used an inflated curve: engine 60 became display 80, 80 became 95 and 90 became 98. Wembanyama OFF 94.4 versus DEF 86.7 therefore compared incompatible units: underlying OFF was 79.1. All player headline and subrating fields now use the same direct bounded model scale, rounded to one decimal. Within the supported 1–99 display domain, equal numerical distances remain equal through the high end. Out-of-range source values remain bounded; this does not establish separation beyond 99.

These are **model grades, not probabilities or percentiles**. Different composites describe different jobs; ten points of shooting and ten points of defense need not have identical possession value. OVR retains its existing role blend and minutes contribution. Its lower display is renumbering, not an ability penalty. Team signals retain rotation/age/depth/context contributions and league ranks. Efficiency charts, zone percentages and contextual spatial suppression retain their own labeled units.

`save.py` owns the common conversion. Cards, Finder, league traits, fantasy details and extension previews advertise `model_skill_1_99`; `play.py` explains units, role blend and interior offense. Finder/Wants compare the returned grades. Autogenerated old-unit Wants cutoffs translate as follows: youth OVR 72/76 → 52/56; non-DEF skill 78 → 58; OVR 75 → 55; injury OVR 73 → 53; budget OVR 70 → 50. Already-direct DEF 78 stays 78. Human requests use current units, without legacy migration. One-point upgrade/near-match tolerances remain in grade points; their old compressed high-end interpretation is not retained as a hidden scale.

Streamed opponent rows now supply explicit `display_ratings` from their already-created attributes, plus scale metadata. Players absent from the browser Finder cache bypass its old fallback curve without a second attribute evaluation or GUI edit. All 12 inspected actual stream rows match the bounded attributes. Fantasy AI already uses engine growth/ceiling/quality; extension comparisons now start on the common scale.
