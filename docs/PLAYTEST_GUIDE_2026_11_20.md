# Playtest guide: what's new and what to look at

_Final, 2026-11-20. Everything below is in the main game. Start a **new save**: old saves don't get the new systems._

## How to start
Run the game the usual way (`nba-gm-data-gui`) and create a **new save**. New games use the newer 94-input ratings. A good first pass: play one full season with your team, through the trade deadline, playoffs, lottery, draft, re-signing and free agency, into the next preseason.

## What's new, and what to check

### Players and ratings
- **The player card** shows the game's real value on the overall/offense/defense numbers: Jokić, Gilgeous-Alexander and Wembanyama near the top. *Check:* does the top 20 feel right?
- Ratings follow analysts first, impact data second. Dunks, the shot types, strength and jumping come from real data.
- Screen assists are a new stat in box scores and leaders. *Check:* do good screeners (Gobert, Sabonis, Allen) lead?
- Prospect cards show "Scouts' upside estimate". True potential stays hidden until a player signs.

### Games, minutes and health
- **Minutes look real.** Stars play about 34–36. Blowouts rest starters, foul trouble sits players, and injured players' minutes go to the right backups. Each coach has a "ride the stars" tendency, shown on the coach card.
- Injuries happen mid-game, and players come back rusty on a minutes limit.
- **Fatigue** builds with minutes actually played and fades with rest days. *Check:* do heavy-minute starters' fatigue bars move?
- **Rest:**
  - AI teams rest stars on back-to-backs, against weak or elite opponents, and once seeds are locked, each time with a stated reason.
  - Your team has a **"Coach rests players" switch** (off by default) and a **zZ "rest for N games" button** next to each player's minutes.
  - *Check:* the zZ suggestion and its reason.
- **National TV games** get a small "TV" tag on the calendar (about 245 a season, picked by roster, stars and market). Teams rest regulars less in those games, as real teams do.
- Seasons open with some players already hurt (offseason surgeries, camp injuries). Players 30 and over rest more on back-to-backs.

### Trades
- AI teams trade for stated reasons: their targets, rebuild sales, tax savings, salary dumps paid in picks, and three-team deals. Trades follow the real calendar: busy draft nights (including chains), the first days of free agency, an August and September market, and the deadline. About 80 trades over two seasons (real is about 110).
- Draft-night trades happen across the draft, including chains.
- **Offers to your team** show their reason, and some ask you to absorb a contract for picks.
- *Check:* do trades around the league make sense? Is the deadline busy enough (real: about 23 trades in deadline week)? Does anyone get traded too often?

### Draft
- Two boards: **Consensus** and **Your board** (your scouts, talent only, updated monthly), plus "Fit for us".
- AI teams pick from their own scouts' board plus their team needs, with no dice. Each pick shows a reason in the draft summary.
- The draft-night board is redesigned, and each pick animates once.
- *Check:* do prospects fall for believable reasons?

### Free agency
- **One free-agency room:** a market table, an offer builder with a live five-season cap table, and a signing wire.
- Free agents judge your offers like everyone else's: you pay close to the market price and can lose a player to a better bid. Stars ask star money (max contracts follow the real 25/30/35% rule), teams keep their own free agents with real Bird rights (over the cap, a fifth year, bigger raises; you get the same), stars are almost always kept, and rebuilding teams don't overpay veterans over 30. A newly acquired player can't be flipped for six months.
- AI teams manage payroll against one real tax line ($200.4M in 2026-27), and teams under it share the tax payout.
- *Check:* does signing feel fair and tense?
- **Contract extensions** (from a player's card) show his real price, a live "Will he sign?" answer with the reason, and a season-by-season cap table with the tax bill or payout. *Check:* does the price feel right?
- The Assistant GM phone tucks into the corner when it would cover an open window; hover to bring it back. "Fits our room" now also needs an open roster spot.

### Screens
- All main tables share one style and sort by click or keyboard.
- Every window has its own icon in the window switcher.
- At playoff entry, Playoff Stats, Minutes and the new **Playoff Injuries** card open beside the bracket.
- The roster cut-down card is a proper table. It only applies from training camp onward, never mid-draft.
- A **year-end recap** after the Finals and a **preseason outlook** at the start of each season; reopen both from Settings. New awards: Finals MVP, Sixth Man, Most Improved and All-NBA, all using the 65-game rule.

## Known gaps (don't spend time on these)
- A simulated month takes about 30–37 seconds.
- The very best players still play a few more games than real ones (too few short 2–10 game injuries).
- There are no counter-asks in trades yet, and no front-office personality. The trade deadline is about half as busy as real.
- Free-agent prices below starter level still come from minutes played. There are no restricted free-agent offer sheets yet (teams simply keep good young players).
- Some screens are only checked in Chrome at desktop sizes.

## How to send feedback
Note what felt wrong and roughly when (date in the game, team, player). Screenshots help. The most useful feedback is anything that doesn't feel like real basketball.
