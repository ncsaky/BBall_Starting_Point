## Decision Quality Test

A defensible decision does not require proving every alternative was bad. It requires showing that the selected tradeoff matched the constraints at the time, naming its costs, and defining evidence that would justify revisiting it.

See [[Learning/Interview Defense Guide]] for how to explain these decisions without overselling them.
