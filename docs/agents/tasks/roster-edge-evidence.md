# NBA roster-edge evidence, 2023–2026

## TL;DR

- A sign-and-trade lets a club sign its departing free agent and immediately trade him. There were 22 across four summers, including five contracts mainly used to make another trade fit the salary rules.
- A two-way deal lets a player split time between the NBA and its development league. Most clubs opened with all three slots occupied. Most promotions came in February or later.
- An Exhibit 10 is a camp contract that can turn into a two-way deal or pay an affiliate bonus. The typical club recorded seven or eight of these signings, and few made the opening roster.
- Camp cuts move clubs from up to 21 players to at most 15 standard players plus three two-ways. Most cuts were on camp contracts, and other clubs claimed six players.
- Most veterans who signed a regular NBA deal did so by August 1. The ones who left for a foreign league mostly had played limited NBA minutes.

A player can earn the bonus after being waived and spending the required 60 days in service with the affiliate. The public records show games played and in some cases the bonus offered. They do not give leaguewide dates of uninterrupted service, the actual amount paid or whether a particular deal even included the optional bonus. There were **no verified payments** in this collection. Affiliate appearances are a measure of follow-through after camp. [Grand Rapids on the bonus](https://grandrapids.gleague.nba.com/faqs) · [G League stat ledger example](https://www.basketball-reference.com/gleague/years/gleague_2026_per_game.html)

The fit picked prior NBA experience in all four tests. The average hit rates across conversions and other outcomes were 64%, 49%, 69% and 78%. In 2026 the rule got 208 of 252 outcomes right, and predicting no conversions got 241 of 252 right with a 50% average hit rate across the two outcomes. Prior NBA experience can help rank candidates. Convert sparingly when a slot opens and a player has a clear role. ## Sign-and-trades

| Summer | Players | Receiving teams | Predicted players from other three summers |

| 2024 | 11 | 8 | 3.67 |

The other summers' average misses by 3.67 players per summer. The public ledger gives the completed deals, so it cannot score a rule for talks that failed or signings that might have used a trade. The 2024 spike included five salary-matching contracts for larger trades. The Towns trade alone accounted for three. Allow the trade when the other team has real matching salary or trade credit and there is value to the departing team. A club with sufficient room can still use the route to secure a restricted free agent or complete a bigger deal. [2023 trade log](https://www.hoopsrumors.com/2023/06/2023-nba-offseason-trades.html) · [2024](https://www.hoopsrumors.com/2024/06/2024-nba-offseason-trades.html) · [2025](https://www.hoopsrumors.com/2025/06/2025-nba-offseason-trades.html) · [2026 NBA trade log](https://www.nba.com/news/2026-offseason-trade-tracker)

Every listed receiving club took on the first-apron hard cap, a firm upper limit on its payroll for the season. The team's ability to take a contract comes from its salary-matching and exception ledger at the moment of the transaction. Dallas used a trade for Klay Thompson after running out of a big enough direct signing route. Washington used a trade credit for Jonas Valančiūnas to preserve its signing budget. Houston had cap room when it took Dillon Brooks in a larger trade. The Lakers used cap room and sent draft picks to secure restricted free agent Walker Kessler. The exact cap balances at the time of some trades remain unresolved. Tre Mann moved on his existing deal as part of Peyton Watson's trade. The 2026 count is four. When a team over the cap re-signs its own player with eligible Bird rights for a sufficiently large raise, base-year compensation can make his outgoing trade salary smaller than what the new team pays. Grant Williams counted for $6.20 million outgoing and $12.41 million incoming. Nickeil Alexander-Walker's reported first salary was $15.16 million, implying $7.58 million on Minnesota's side under the same conditions. The data distinguish this inference from a documented calculation. The status stays unresolved where the signing team's cap position or rights are unclear. ## Resume state

All five decisions are recorded. Run `python3 scripts/research/roster_edge_fit.py` from the repository to repeat the fits. Each fold selects a rule on three summers and scores the fourth. Average hit rates are the mean of the hit rates for the two outcomes, so rare choices count as much as common ones. Research only, on `codex/roster-edge-evidence`.
