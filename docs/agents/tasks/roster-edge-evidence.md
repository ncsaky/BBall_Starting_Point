# NBA roster-edge evidence, 2023–2026

## TL;DR

- A sign-and-trade lets a club sign its departing free agent and immediately trade him. There were 22 across four summers, including five contracts mainly used to make another trade fit the salary rules.
- A two-way deal lets a player split time between the NBA and its development league. Signings, promotions and active-game limits are being checked.
- An Exhibit 10 is a camp contract that can turn into a two-way deal or pay an affiliate bonus. Camp outcomes are being checked.
- Camp cuts move clubs from up to 21 players to at most 15 standard players plus three two-ways. Opening rosters and waiver claims are being checked.
- Veterans who sign late or leave for another league help show how quickly the summer market thins. Those moves are being checked.

The other summers' average misses by 3.67 players per summer. The public ledger gives the completed deals, so it cannot score a rule for talks that failed or signings that might have used a trade. The 2024 spike included five salary-matching contracts for larger trades. The Towns trade alone accounted for three. Allow the trade when the other team has real matching salary or trade credit and there is value to the departing team. A club with sufficient room can still use the route to secure a restricted free agent or complete a bigger deal. Every listed receiving club took on the first-apron hard cap, a firm upper limit on its payroll for the season. The team's ability to take a contract comes from its salary-matching and exception ledger at the moment of the transaction. Dallas used a trade for Klay Thompson after running out of a big enough direct signing route. Washington used a trade credit for Jonas Valančiūnas to preserve its signing budget. Tre Mann moved on his existing deal as part of Peyton Watson's trade. The 2026 count is four. [2024 examples](https://www.hoopsrumors.com/2024/08/hoops-rumors-glossary-sign-and-trade.html) · [2025 hard caps](https://www.hoopsrumors.com/2025/07/nba-teams-with-hard-caps-for-2025-26.html) · [Player agreement](https://nbpa.com/cba/)

When a team over the cap re-signs its own player with eligible Bird rights for a sufficiently large raise, base-year compensation can make his outgoing trade salary smaller than what the new team pays. Grant Williams counted for $6.20 million outgoing and $12.41 million incoming. Nickeil Alexander-Walker's reported first salary was $15.16 million, implying $7.58 million on Minnesota's side under the same conditions. The data distinguish this inference from a documented calculation. The status stays unresolved where the signing team's cap position or rights are unclear.
