# NBA roster-edge evidence, 2023–2026

## TL;DR

- A two-way deal lets a player split time between the NBA and its development league. Most clubs opened with all three slots occupied. Most promotions came in February or later.
- An Exhibit 10 is a camp contract that can turn into a two-way deal or pay an affiliate bonus. The typical club recorded seven or eight of these signings, and few made the opening roster.
- Camp cuts move clubs from up to 21 players to at most 15 standard players plus three two-ways. Most cuts were on camp contracts, and other clubs claimed six players.
- A sign-and-trade lets a club sign its departing free agent and immediately trade him. There were 22 across four summers, including five contracts mainly used to make another trade fit the salary rules.
- Most veterans who signed a regular NBA deal did so by August 1. The ones who left for a foreign league mostly had played limited NBA minutes.

The other summers' average misses by 3.67 players per summer. The public ledger gives the completed deals, so it cannot score a rule for talks that failed or signings that might have used a trade. The 2024 spike included five salary-matching contracts for larger trades. The Towns trade alone accounted for three. Charlotte used part of its signing budget for teams with cap room, taking the players along with picks and cash. The Knicks needed enough outgoing salary to bring in Towns. Allow the trade when the other team has enough legal spending room or trade credit and there is value to the departing team. A club with sufficient room can still use the route to secure a restricted free agent or complete a bigger deal. Every listed receiving club took on the first-apron hard cap, a firm upper limit on its payroll for the season. The team's ability to take a contract comes from its salary-matching and exception ledger at the moment of the transaction. Dallas used a trade for Klay Thompson after running out of a big enough direct signing route. Washington used a trade credit for Jonas Valančiūnas to preserve its signing budget. Houston had cap room when it took Dillon Brooks in a larger trade. The Lakers used cap room and sent draft picks to secure restricted free agent Walker Kessler. The exact cap balances at the time of some trades remain unresolved. Tre Mann moved on his existing deal as part of Peyton Watson's trade. The 2026 count is four. [2024 examples](https://www.hoopsrumors.com/2024/08/hoops-rumors-glossary-sign-and-trade.html) · [Houston trade release](https://www.nba.com/rockets/news/rockets-acquire-dillon-brooks-as-part-of-five-team-trade) · [Lakers cap reporting](https://www.silverscreenandroll.com/lakers-analysis/121083/lakers-nba-free-agency-salary-cap-sheet-money-contract-analysis) · [2025 hard caps](https://www.hoopsrumors.com/2025/07/nba-teams-with-hard-caps-for-2025-26.html) · [Player agreement](https://nbpa.com/cba/)

When a team over the cap re-signs its own player using eligible rights for a sufficiently large raise, base-year compensation sets his outgoing trade salary to the greater of his old salary and half his new starting salary. The receiving team counts the new salary. Three documented examples are Max Strus ($7.24 million outgoing, $14.49 million incoming), Grant Williams ($6.20 million and $12.41 million) and Dillon Brooks ($11.40 million and $22.63 million in base pay). The data also mark two inferred cases. Nickeil Alexander-Walker's $15.16 million first salary implies $7.58 million on Minnesota's side. Peyton Watson's $20.47 million implies $10.23 million for Denver. Some signing-day cap balances and contract rights remain unresolved. ## Resume state

All five decisions are recorded. Data rows reference a source registry with publication, observation or access dates. Run `python3 scripts/research/roster_edge_fit.py` from the repository to repeat the fits. Each fold selects a rule on three summers and scores the fourth. Average hit rates are the mean of the hit rates for the two outcomes, so rare choices count as much as common ones. Research only, on `codex/roster-edge-evidence`.
