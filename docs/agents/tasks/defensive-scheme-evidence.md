# DEFENSIVE-SCHEME-EVIDENCE

## TL;DR

- The public evidence supports a league switch rate and a few directly tracked matchup examples.
- Team and player matchup summaries can show who guarded whom over the year.
- Ball-screen coverage depends on the two players involved and can change within a game.
- Help, zone and off-ball screening need their own measurements.
- This research is for the Stage 2 coach and possession model.

## 1. Pick-and-roll coverage

A drop keeps the screener's defender closer to the basket than the screen. At-level coverage brings that defender up to contain the ball near the screen. A show or hedge sends them briefly toward the ball before they recover. A blitz commits two defenders to the ball. A switch trades assignments. Ice tries to deny a side screen and send the handler toward the sideline or baseline. Ice describes the ball defender's direction and can coexist with a dropping big. Coverage can also change after the first call.
