# NBA staff turnover evidence

## TL;DR

- The head-coach ledger covers all 510 team-seasons from 2009–10 to 2025–26.
- It tracks firings, contract nonrenewals, other departures, and temporary appointments.
- The first fit checks how much preseason expectations and tenure tell us about summer firings.
- Assistant and front-office evidence collection is in progress.
- This is research for the staff market and lifecycle.
