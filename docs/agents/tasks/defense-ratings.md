## What changed and why

- **Rotation reads.** Help Effectiveness is now a weak outcome check and appears once. Help Defense Talent is strongly associated with help activity. Among roster players with at least 600 minutes, their within-season rank correlations were **0.84 / 0.85 / 0.83** across 2023–24 to 2025–26. The broader help grade can identify real contributors, yet part of its signal is how often a defender is in the scheme’s help role. Help Effectiveness only observes disruption once help is offered and repeated at 0.20. Separating the two keeps help frequency in the help tendency. Basketball Index’s film discussion also distinguishes help instincts and assignments from broad impact. [Basketball Index’s 2025 All-Defensive teams](https://www.bball-index.com/basketball-indexs-2025-all-defensive-teams/) · [Basketball Index’s role and rim-defense scouting](https://www.bball-index.com/lakers-nuggets-scouting-report/)
- **Off-ball positioning.** The same Help Effectiveness outcome previously supplied both positioning and rotation reads. Reads can be late even when a player recovers with length, and the starting position can be correct even when a teammate breaks the coverage. The positioning input now uses the existing estimate and any specific film claims.
- **Attention to assignments.** Defensive three-second violations per possession rewarded perimeter assignments that seldom encounter the rule. In both 2024–25 and 2025–26, the median was 0.01 per 75 for point-of-attack defenders versus 0.045–0.05 for anchor bigs. Around four in five point-of-attack defenders were at 0.01 or less, compared with about one in five anchor bigs in 2025–26. The NBA rule depends on time in the lane and whether a defender is actively guarding. The capture has possessions but no denominator of exposed paint time. The build stops using those calls as evidence of assignment awareness. [NBA Rule 10, Section VII](https://official.nba.com/rule-no-10-violations-and-penalties/)
- **Attributed film.** Wolfond informs Kuminga’s rotation reads and off-ball positioning. Walton informs his attention and screen technique. Raj Chipalu’s film of Dorian Finney-Smith on the Lakers supports stronger weak-side positioning, with his smaller Houston sample kept in view. The normal analyst step applies the existing single-analyst weight of 0.60. Article titles, authors, dates, URLs and section locators are kept in the claim record and in the resulting input’s evidence. [Chipalu, May 27, 2025](https://www.silverscreenandroll.com/2025/5/27/24437717/dorian-finney-smith-lakers-season-review-film-breakdown-plus-minus-trade-luka-doncic)
