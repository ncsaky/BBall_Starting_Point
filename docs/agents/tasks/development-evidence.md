# NBA player development evidence

## TL;DR

- The study follows NBA regular seasons ending in 2005–2026.
- Basketball Reference supplies minutes, player ages, box-score impact and draft picks.
- Basketball Index supplies a second measure of on-court impact for newer seasons.
- Growth estimates follow the same players across adjacent seasons.
- Star odds use equal follow-up windows, with newer eligible cohorts reserved for testing.

## Resume state

Research only, on `codex/development-evidence`. Public Basketball Reference advanced and draft tables are being cached under `output/development-evidence/`. The Basketball Index progression and LEBRON captures and the Python environment are available via local symlinks. The baseline and held-out design follow the offseason decisions report. The intended game comparison is the early plateau and falling top end in the ten-season league health check.
