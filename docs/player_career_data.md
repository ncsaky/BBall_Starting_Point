# Player career season data

Source: ESPN public regular-season athlete statistics and annual NBA standings. Imported October 14, 2026.

Reviewed 691 game players; matched 679 athlete histories; 572 have returned NBA season records (3089 rows).

## Rules

- NBA regular seasons only; postseason and college records are not mixed in.
- A traded season uses the source combined total once, or aggregates its individual stints if no total is supplied.
- Shooting percentages use made/attempted totals. Minutes use published per-game figures and retain source rounding.
- Games-played denominators use that season’s team standings, including shortened seasons. Traded-season available schedules are left unknown rather than summing both teams’ full schedules.
- Save-owned current and completed seasons supersede imported data from the save’s initial season onward.
- Completed simulated seasons remain in the existing season_history snapshots; the career dropdown reads those snapshots after each rollover.
- Unknown draft information and missing NBA records are not invented.
