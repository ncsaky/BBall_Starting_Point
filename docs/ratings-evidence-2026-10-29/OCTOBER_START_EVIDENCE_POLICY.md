## How to use the retained evidence

| Evidence | Treatment in the next rating review |
|---|---|
| Completed prior-season performance, known by the October start | Eligible evidence of demonstrated ability. Preserve season, sample and role. |
| Offseason scouting or podcast assessment published before the October start | Eligible, broadly applicable assessment. Check material intervening events; separate observation from prediction. |
| Forecast of a breakout, ceiling or expanded role | Record as projection with uncertainty; do not present it as demonstrated current performance. |
| Injury, trade, signing or role change before the October start | Reconcile the specific affected health, roster, contract or role claim. Reassess skill only when the evidence supports doing so. |
| Opening-week or later performance | Keep explicitly post-start. It may inform a later update or contextual review; it is not information known at the pre-opener start. |
| July-dated or otherwise older canonical input | Keep its actual provenance visible and compare against the October target state. Age alone does not establish a wrong trait; a displayed October date does not establish refreshed inputs. |

No blanket retraction or retranscription of the offseason episodes is needed. Repeated opinions by the same speakers are not independent corroboration, and podcast rank does not set OVR. Preserve the transcript-only coverage and speaker-identification limits in the existing ledger.

## Bounded next increment

Continue the existing player-level review using the retained evidence and the game's current scale. For each proposed change, record only:

1. The raw trait and verified baseline/version being evaluated, with any card output labeled by its capture date. Historical November 2 presentation values are not automatically current after subsequent scale changes.
2. Dated demonstrated strengths, weaknesses and counterevidence, plus any forecast kept separate.
3. A material-event check through the October start: verified event and source, or **not checked / unresolved**. Do not claim absence of an event from silence in a transcript.
4. A same-scale peer comparison, proposed trait target or justified hold, confidence, and expected OFF/DEF/OVR direction.

An unresolved event should hold only the affected conclusion. For example, an uncertain return date holds the injury-adjusted effective-value conclusion, while healthy shooting or passing evidence can still be assessed. A trade changes team context and may change role; it does not by itself prove a skill change. Do not infer prospect growth or potential from podcast placement.
