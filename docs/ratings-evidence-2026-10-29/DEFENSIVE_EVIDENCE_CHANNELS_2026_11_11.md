### Covariance / circularity rules for a reusable import

Group evidence by origin: defended-shot outcomes; matchup/action exposure; box/hustle events; physical tools; impact; scouting. Track provider, season, as-of date, denominator, definition and whether a field is measured, modeled or subjective. A populated field with inherited confidence.82 is not an82% probability of correct elite ability.

* Choose a provider composite **or** a deliberate combination of its component evidence. Do not add the provider's total rim score on top of all of its prevention/activity/disruption components as if unrelated.
* Use DPM/D-LEBRON/team defense as external checks or explicitly limited priors. Broad impact in rim, agility, effort, navigation and IQ creates repeated credit; an offense-containing measure such as DARKO adds another contamination risk.
* Separate **observed jobs** from ability. A workbook “Rim Protector” label cannot award16 ability points, and guarding varied positions cannot prove that the assignments succeeded. A role-conditioned evaluation may account for opportunity without granting skill for the label.
* Use tools once. If defended-shot success already measures the effect of reach, adding full height credit again double counts it. Where shot evidence is missing, a size prior may be useful but must remain a prior, with uncertainty and exceptions. Do not subtract a second height penalty from an already size-adjusted small guard estimate.
* Keep missingness separate from talent. Use a broad disclosed prior or range; do not assign zero or multiply skill by a confidence fraction. Evidence gaps should limit confidence more than force every low-minute player below every starter.

### Stable league-wide application

Freeze a multi-season reference set and its season/competition rules before selecting anchors. Use possessions/opportunities and explicit uncertainty; retain raw sample denominators. Fit/choose the shared mapping on that reference, with a finite rare tail, then apply it unchanged to new entrants. Avoid live min/max scaling, “best player=100,” annual rank-to-grade conversion and post-import league percentile remapping. Reference players are checks, not name-triggered gameplay rules.

For a populated independent channel, estimate skill conditional on opportunity/context and stabilize noisy samples. For missing channels, apply a broad documented prior from relevant observed evidence, not the current job label alone. Do not guarantee precision for every fringe player or force all priors to be manual. Inspect the small number of rule violations/outliers and a stratified sample; broad rules should handle most players. Backend owns formula selection, reconstruction, sensitivity and distribution checks, so this research does not duplicate those experiments.

Two forms of headroom matter: raw model estimates must not clip all high priors to100 before useful evidence is considered, and final effective grading must preserve distinctions without stretching arbitrary percentiles into measured skill. Uncapped values can reveal the first issue; they cannot establish the second. A new reference version may change calibration deliberately, but an incoming rookie must not silently move existing players' anchors.
