## Reference environments and intended ordering

These are clear integration contrasts, not instructions to run simulations in this research lane or predetermined final scores:

1. **Available paint contest:** standardized attacker/action, workload and starting opportunity; C27 combines S36/S37/S45 with actual/uncertain tools. Wembanyama/Gobert are high effective coverage anchors versus guard controls. Zubac's conventional drop value and Turner's block-capable profile should remain plausible without equating every tall/rim100 player. Do not impose an exact Wembanyama>Gobert technique ranking.
2. **Coverage from a declared starting position:** starting depth/angle and travel determine whether a contest becomes available. Bam's mobile coverage, Zubac's conventional drop, Barnes's help and Draymond's support hypothesis need distinguishable profiles. A shorter helper can recognize/execute better while reaching less court than a long anchor. No role label grants ability.
3. **Weak-side help and recovery:** S38 recognition, S39 starting position, physical travel and S40 controlled arrival are distinct causes. White/Barnes versus Castle is a supported profile contrast, with uncertain exact attribution. Late arrival does not automatically mean slow physical tools; correct recognition does not guarantee timely execution.
4. **Technique-only contrast at equal tools:** hold physical reports/priors and exposure constant to inspect technical effects; then restore different tools to evaluate coverage. A guard's excellent timing must not automatically become center-sized shot prevention, and height must not automatically become good timing.
5. **Legal execution and choice:** S45 affects legal contest execution; T16 chooses jump/stay-down/commit behavior. Foul rate depends on opponent/action and tactical decisions, so don't force the same inferred tendency and technique from one rate. P11/P12 workload response is separate from T17 willingness.
6. **Possession security and support:** S30/S31/S32 with tools/assignments account for defensive collection and teammate box-out benefits. A strong anchor's value is not only blocks; a sheltered guard still has closeout, tag, communication and rebound obligations. Low-demand compatibility R20 is not elite overall defense.

Numeric formulas, time/geometry constants, reference OFF/DEF/OVR and role weights need verification by the backend. No bonus is added for C36 after C27/C32 supplied the same contest/prevention mechanism. No new standalone recovery ratings, global IQ multiplier or second independently editable talent model is proposed.
