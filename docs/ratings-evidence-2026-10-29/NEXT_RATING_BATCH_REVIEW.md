## Shared mapping problems and approval boundaries

**Guard versus big defensive weighting.** In [save.py](../../src/nba_gm_data/save.py), player_attribute_summary averages effort, scheme IQ, navigation and rim deterrence equally for headline DEF; lateral agility does not enter that mean. In [sim.py](../../src/nba_gm_data/sim.py), activity instead uses .42 effort + .36 lateral + .22 navigation, alongside a separate nonlinear rim channel. SGA/Curry's headline comparison, Castle/White's helper ordering and Barnes/Flagg's large rim channels warrant a shared role audit. Do not force universal defensive impact ordering with per-player compensations. No formula change is proposed.

**Correlated effort/navigation proxies.** [traits.py](../../src/nba_gm_data/traits.py) infers both from overlapping deflections, steals, impact and role data, rather than independent observations of effort and screen traversal. Cason and White have legitimate qualitative defensive cases, but their relative magnitudes and near-ceiling overlap cannot be proved by the proxies alone. Castle's proposed redistribution preserves a containment specialist while removing the unsupported help-defense implication. Brown's on-ball defense is preserved despite team-awareness criticism.

**Saturation and high-end compression.** Sim nonlinear_high caps at99; Gobert, Kessler and Allen all reach the rim ceiling. Token reductions could alter the card while leaving the principal rim channel unchanged. Presentation ratings also compress the top end. Barnes/Flagg helper grades need joint comparison with centers, not opportunistic blanket cuts in this batch. Display numbers are not direct gameplay probabilities.

**Nonspacing specialist offense.** [offense.py](../../src/nba_gm_data/offense.py) offensive_profile, support_passing and finishing logic represent screening/interior finishing and feeder support, separately from self-creation. Gobert/Allen/Kessler should finish when served while constraining spacing. Low headline OFF alone does not establish absence of that role. Review feeder/spacing interactions in a separately authorized gameplay check before giving them invented shooting or handling.

**Organizer and off-ball effects.** Durant's pressured handling can be corrected now; the extent to which an organizer prevents that pressure is a lineup mechanic question. Curry has explicit veteran usage/assist adjustments in sim; those do not prove teammate gravity is fully represented. Preserve elite shooting and evaluate team benefits separately.

## Interpretation of changes, source dependencies and gaps

These are player behavior corrections, not desired OVR ranks. Bane/Reaves gain scoring repertoire while retaining separation/playoff doubts. Herro remains a very good jump shooter but should win fewer possessions through downhill force. Brown should create his own shot more reliably than he organizes teammates. Garland should handle more confidently when healthy. Castle should contain a guard more reliably than protect the basket. Paolo retains physical mismatch pressure with less helper/organizer credit. Durant retains efficient shooting with more pressure-induced difficulty.

The JSON records all changed spatial fields. Versatility also improves modeled post creation, assisted finishing and shooting-zone efficiency; it is not a dedicated pull-up switch. Herro's rim-pressure reduction lowers finishing as well as drive threat. Brown/Paolo passing changes affect IQ/creation and simulation passing. Castle's weaker rim channel lowers the rebound/block summaries despite unchanged offensive boards; increased effort raises activity and several spatial contests. Durant/Garland handling changes also move usage through impact/creation. The measured table exposes these coupled effects so the coordinator can approve subparts.
