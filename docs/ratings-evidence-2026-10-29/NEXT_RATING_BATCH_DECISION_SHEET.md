## How to interpret defense and coupled traits

Containment means staying in front of the ball; navigation means recovering through screens; help means recognizing and covering another defender's breakdown; rim deterrence means preventing or worsening interior attempts. White's guard-help account and Castle's containment/help split distinguish actual behaviors. Barnes/Flagg's size and versatile help do not establish center-level deterrence. Gobert/Kessler/Allen offer a distinct rim-anchor role. Duren's positive rim evidence and Kawhi's offensive evidence should remain attached to their actual skills rather than reputation.

Overlapping deflection/steal/impact/role inputs cannot independently establish every one of these skills. A high inferred score does not prove good containment or navigation; a low one does not prove a bad defender. [traits.py](../../src/nba_gm_data/traits.py) documents those proxy families. [save.py](../../src/nba_gm_data/save.py) headline DEF equally averages effort, scheme, navigation and rim, whereas [sim.py](../../src/nba_gm_data/sim.py) separates weighted activity and nonlinear rim influence. The backend owns aligning those meanings and saturation. Castle/Paolo defensive reductions are therefore withheld rather than installed as cosmetic headline corrections. Their genuine role differences still need assessment after the shared change.

Versatility changes also improve modeled post creation, assisted finishing and spatial shooting efficiency; they are not isolated pull-up controls. Herro's rim-pressure correction changes finishing as well as drives/FT pressure. Brown passing moves IQ, creation and simulation passing. Handling changes affect projected usage through creation/impact. The isolated calculation table measures these cross-category effects; combined effects are not assumed additive. All values are fixed canonical-minute crosswalks, not actual runtime cards or projected win totals.
